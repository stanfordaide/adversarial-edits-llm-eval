# Adversarial term dictionary — changelog

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `adversarial_term_dictionary_v1_as_used.json` | The dictionary used to generate every adversarial report and every result in the paper. Use it to reproduce the study. |
| `adversarial_term_dictionary_v2_cleaned.json` | v1 with erroneous alternatives removed. Recommended for reuse. The study was **not** re-run with v2. |

Both files have the same 247 terms in the same categories: anatomy (47), confidence_terms (9), descriptors (63), finding (25), location (38), pathology (57), study_types (8).
Only alternative lists differ.

Format: `{"term_alternatives": {term: {"alternatives": [...], "category": ...}}}`. Measurement substitutions (e.g. "1.5 cm" → "3.3 mm") are generated by rule and are not dictionary entries, which is why the dictionary has seven categories while the paper describes eight.

## v1 → v2: 16 terms changed, 33 alternatives removed, 2 added or corrected

Alternatives that belonged to a different category than their term (e.g. the pathology term `atelectasis` offered for the finding `effusion`, or the descriptor `acute` for the pathology `acute cholecystitis`), or that were not clinical terms (`general`).
